Flywheel Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page applies named ideal-form rules to the numbers you type: half the mass times the radius squared for a solid disc's inertia, half the inertia times the speed squared for the stored energy, and inertia times speed over torque for the spin-up time. The masses, radii, speeds and torques are yours to measure or read off your own drawing. It applies the rules as written, so it has not seen your wheel and does not know the material, the stress at the rim, the bearings or the balance, and it gives no verdict that a wheel spinning at your speed holds together. Every number here is your own entry.

The inertia truth

The inertia of a solid disc: half the mass times the radius squared.

The energy truth

What a spinning inertia stores: half the inertia times the speed squared.

The spin-up truth

How long a constant torque takes to reach a speed.

The average power of delivering an energy over a time.
